### 📥 How to upload a theme .zip file to Shopify

Installing a premium theme on your Shopify store is a straightforward process. Follow these quick steps to get started: 

1. **Log in** to your Shopify Admin Dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Online Store** > **Themes** in the left-hand menu.
3. Scroll down to the **Theme library** section.
4. Click the **Add theme** button and choose **Upload zip file**.
5. Select the raw theme .zip file from your computer and click **Upload file**.

Once uploaded, the theme will appear as a draft in your library. You can customize it freely before publishing it live to your customers.
