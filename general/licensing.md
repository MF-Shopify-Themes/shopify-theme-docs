By purchasing and downloading a theme from this organization, you agree to the following standard licensing terms: 

* **Single-Store License:** Each theme purchase grants you a non-exclusive license to use the theme on a **single live Shopify store**.
* **Development Stores:** You are allowed to install the theme on development or staging environments associated with that specific store for testing purposes.
* **Modifications:** You are free to modify the theme's code to fit your brand identity or functionality needs. However, you cannot resell, redistribute, or sub-license the theme (modified or unmodified) to third parties.
