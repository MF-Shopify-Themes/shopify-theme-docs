### 🚀 Arcanum Theme Installation

Setting up Arcanum on your Shopify store is quick and seamless. Follow these steps to upload, configure, and publish your theme.

---

### 📥 Step 1: Download the Theme Package

When you purchase or receive Arcanum, you will obtain a `.zip` file (e.g., `arcanum-theme-v1.0.0.zip`).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Do not unzip this file.** Shopify requires the theme to be uploaded in its original compressed `.zip` format.

---

### 📤 Step 2: Upload to Shopify

1. Log in to your **Shopify Admin Dashboard**.
2. Navigate to **Online Store** > **Themes** in the left-hand navigation.
3. In the *Theme library* section, click the **Add theme** button and select **Upload zip file**.
4. Click **Browse**, select your `arcanum-theme-v1.0.0.zip` file, and click **Upload file**.

The theme will take a few moments to unpack and will appear in your Theme Library as an unpublished draft.

---

### 🎨 Step 3: Select Your Style Preset & Customize

Before publishing the theme live to your visitors:

1. Click **Customize** next to the Arcanum theme in your library.
2. In the left-hand sidebar, click the **Gear Icon (Theme settings)**.
3. Open the **Style & Preset** dropdown and choose your preferred visual mood:
   * **Dark (Alchimista)** — Deep occult black, gold, and Garamond typography.
   * **Vampire (Gotico Romantico)** — Dramatic crimson and gothic aesthetic.
   * **Runic (Druidico)** — Ancient earth stone, bronze, and auto-runic glyph headings.
   * **Celestial (Olistico di Lusso)** — Ethereal astral blue, gold foil, and luxury sacred geometry.
4. Add your logo, configure your navigation, and add your products.

---

### 🚀 Step 4: Publish

Once your store content, collections, and settings are configured:
1. Go back to **Online Store** > **Themes**.
2. Click **Publish** next to Arcanum to make it your live storefront.

---

### ❓ Troubleshooting

* **"Zip file is invalid" Error:** Ensure you uploaded the exact `.zip` package without extracting it first.
* **Metafield Filters Not Appearing:** Ensure you have configured your storefront filters in the official **Shopify Search & Discovery** app (see our [Sections Guide](./sections.md#metaphysical-filtering)).
