### ⚙️ Arcanum Global Theme Settings

Global settings control the store-wide visual identity, typography, presets, subscription widgets, and cart behavior across your entire storefront. You can access these settings by opening the Shopify Theme Editor and clicking on the **Gear Icon (Theme settings)** in the left sidebar.

---

### 🎨 1. Style & Presets (Instant Visual Switcher)

Arcanum includes 4 fully-realized style presets designed for different metaphysical market segments:

* **Dark (Alchimista — Default)**:
  * *Palette*: Deep Obsidian (`#0A0A0B`), Platinum White (`#F5F5F7`), Soft Silver (`#EDEDED`).
  * *Atmospheric Micro-Animations*: Amazing moving mist and smoke background animation (`.alchemical-smoke-canvas`) powered by native procedural SVG turbulence, and an amazing moving mist and glow animation drifting along collection product card contours at 60 FPS without layout shifts.
  * *Typography*: Cormorant Garamond headings and Lato/EB Garamond body font.
  * *Dividers*: Minimalist transparent-to-white gradient lines.
  * *Best for*: Grimoires, occult bookshops, traditional alchemy, tarot publishers.

* **Vampire (Gotico Romantico)**:
  * *Palette*: Dark Graphite (`#121214`), Arterial Crimson (`#A30014`), Ash Grey (`#E2D9D9`).
  * *Typography*: Dramatic elongated Tulpen One headings with Cinzel body text.
  * *Dividers*: Skewed blood-drip procedural CSS dividers.
  * *Best for*: Gothic jewelry, dark altar supplies, consecrated ritual oils, dark romance literature.

* **Runic (Druidico)**:
  * *Palette*: Warm Stone Grey (`#242528`), Antique Bronze Gold (`#D4AF37`), Ancient Parchment (`#DCD6CD`).
  * *Typography*: Caesar Dressing headings with automated character-level runic substitutions (`render-runic-text`) and alternating carved offsets.
  * *Accessibility*: 100% accessible via parent `aria-label` and `aria-hidden` attributes for screen readers and SEO crawlers.
  * *Best for*: Nordic pagan crafts, rune sets, herbalism, wood altar tools.

* **Celestial (Olistico di Lusso)**:
  * *Palette*: Astral Ice Blue (`#F4F7F9`), Smoked Slate (`#4A4F55`), Solar Gold (`#B8860B`).
  * *Typography*: Tenor Sans body with uppercase gold Cinzel headings with celestial starlight drop-shadows.
  * *Best for*: Fine crystal jewelry, modern astrology salons, aura essences, luxury orgonite.

### 🖼️ 2. Logo & Brand Identity

* **Logo Image (`logo`)**: Upload your brand logomark (SVG, WebP, PNG, JPG).
* **Logo Width**: Customize desktop and mobile header display widths.
* **Favicon**: Upload a square icon (e.g., alchemical symbol, pentacle, crystal) for browser tabs.

---

### 🔁 3. Subscriptions & Mystery Boxes

Configure global settings for recurring monthly mystery boxes and ritual restocks powered by native Shopify Selling Plans:
* **Show Subscription Savings Callout**: Toggle display of percentage savings badges (e.g., *"Save 15% + Free Monthly Crystal"*).
* **Delivery Frequency Labels**: Customize wording for subscription intervals (*Every New Moon*, *Every 30 Days*, *Quarterly Solstice*).

---

### 🛒 4. Cart & Altar Drawer Options

* **Cart Type**: Choose between **Slide-Out Altar Drawer** or standard page.
* **Tiered Consecration Bar**: Enable dynamic free gift / free shipping progress threshold (e.g., *"Add $15 more for Free Consecrated Incense"*).
* **Quick Altar Upsells**: Enable 1-click add-on items inside the cart drawer (velvet pouches, matches, charcoal discs, sage bundles).

---

* 🇬🇧 **English** (`en` - Default)
* 🇮🇹 **Italian** (`it`)
* 🇩🇪 **German** (`de`)
* 🇫🇷 **French** (`fr`)

* 🇵🇹 **Portuguese** (`pt`)
* 🇳🇱 **Dutch** (`nl`)
* 🇷🇺 **Russian** (`ru`)
* 🇯🇵 **Japanese** (`ja`)

Manage target languages in **Shopify Admin > Settings > Languages** and enable the native Language & Currency Selector in your Header/Footer.
