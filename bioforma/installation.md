### 🚀 BioForma Theme Installation

Setting up BioForma on your Shopify store is quick and easy. Follow these simple steps to upload and publish your theme. 

### 📥 Step 1: Download the Theme Package

When you purchase or receive BioForma, you will get a .zip file (e.g., bioforma-theme-v1.0.0.zip). 

* **Important:** Do not un-zip this file. Shopify requires the theme to be uploaded in its original compressed .zip format.

### 📤 Step 2: Upload to Shopify

1. Log in to your **Shopify Admin Dashboard**.
2. Go to **Online Store** > **Themes** in the left-hand menu.
3. In the *Theme library* section, click the **Add theme** button and select **Upload zip file**.
4. Click **Browse**, select your bioforma-theme-v1.0.0.zip file, and click **Upload file**.

The theme will take a few moments to install and will appear in your Theme Library as a draft. 

### 🎨 Step 3: Customize & Publish

Before making the theme visible to your customers, you can customize it: 

1. Click **Customize** next to the BioForma theme to open the Shopify Editor.
2. Once your sections, colors, and products are ready, go back to the Theme Library.
3. Click **Publish** to make BioForma the live theme for your store.

### ❓ Troubleshooting

* **"Zip file is invalid" Error:** This usually happens if you accidentally compressed the wrong folder or unzipped the package. Make sure you are uploading the raw theme .zip file directly.
* **Upload Limit:** Ensure your file size does not exceed Shopify's theme upload limits (currently 50MB).
